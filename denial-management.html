Questions about denial management

Appeals include clinical documentation, so they are prepared only inside HIPAA-compliant systems under a BAA.

Can you work only our old denials? ›

Yes! We can perform a one-time backlog recovery project to size, audit, and appeal your existing denied claims before they pass timely filing deadlines.

Which denials can still be recovered? ›

Denials caused by coding errors, missing prior authorizations, medical necessity disputes, and modifier issues can usually be appealed successfully if worked within the payer's timely filing window.

What are the most common denial reasons? ›

The most frequent denial causes include registration/eligibility errors, lack of prior authorization, inaccurate ICD-10 or CPT coding, non-covered service codes, and duplicate claims.
