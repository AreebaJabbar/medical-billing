Medical Billing and Transcription Services for Healthcare Providers

At Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription Pvt. Ltd., we work with healthcare providers to manage medical billing and clinical documentation. Our team handles transcription, coding, editing and proofreading, helping clinics and hospitals manage their day-to-day work while giving clinicians more time to focus on patients.

We can work with your existing facility system or provide access to our subscription EMR/EHR for dictation and related services. If you have questions about our services or how we can fit into your workflow, get in touch with our team.

Our Services

01

Medical Transcription

Our medical transcription team works with clinical documents from different medical specialties. We use a range of EMR and EHR systems and can review and edit both voice-recognition transcripts and AI-generated drafts to make sure the final documents are clear and ready for review.

We use a quality-check process to help maintain accurate documentation, with reported accuracy of up to 99%. For certain AI-assisted workflows, turnaround time has been reduced from 24 hours to as little as 6 hours.

03

Revenue Cycle Management (RCM)

Managing billing from patient registration to final payment takes time and attention. Our medical billing and coding team helps healthcare practices prepare claims, follow up on unpaid accounts and review denied claims so billing issues can be addressed promptly.

Protecting patient information is an important part of medical billing. We follow HIPAA-related procedures and review claims and account activity carefully. Our team also follows up on outstanding payments and billing issues, helping your staff spend less time managing administrative tasks.

Our RCM services include:

  • Medical coding and billing
  • Provider credentialing
  • Prior authorization support
  • Insurance verification and eligibility
  • Claims management
  • Denial management
  • Payment posting
  • Accounts receivable follow-up
  • Billing audits and analysis

AAPC Certifications: CPC® and CPB®

Through its strategic partnership with the American Academy of Professional Coders (AAPC), Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription helps students in Pakistan access training for two internationally recognized credentials: Certified Professional Coder (CPC®) and Certified Professional Biller (CPB®).

AAPC provides the training and credentials. Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription supports students throughout the enrollment process by coordinating batch schedules, payment processing, and access to required books and online learning resources.

Questions about CPC® and CPB®

All FAQs →
Who teaches the courses?

Courses are taught live online by AAPC-certified instructors and faculty.

Is the training online or in person?

The training is 100% live online, allowing you to learn from anywhere with AAPC faculty.

Who awards the certification?

The certification is awarded directly by AAPC upon passing the official exam.

What does Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription do?

As AAPC's Strategic Partner in Pakistan, Capital Links supports students throughout enrollment, batch coordination, payment processing, and access to books and learning materials.

Which course should I choose?

If you want to focus on medical coding, choose CPC®. For medical billing, choose CPB®. If you want comprehensive knowledge across both, choose the CPC® + CPB® dual certification package.

How much does it cost?

CPC® is USD 1,050, CPB® is USD 1,050, and the dual CPC® + CPB® package is USD 1,800 (saving USD 300).

Do I need a medical background?

No prior medical background is required. The courses cover fundamental medical terminology and anatomy as part of the curriculum.

How do I register for the CPC exam?

Capital Links coordinates your exam registration directly with AAPC as part of your course package.

How do I maintain my certification?

Certifications are maintained by earning Continuing Education Units (CEUs) through AAPC membership.
