About Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription

A Pakistan-based team preparing to support healthcare providers with medical billing, coding, and transcription.

Our Story

Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription (SMC) Pvt. Ltd. was established in Pakistan in 2024. Our aim is to help healthcare teams manage documentation and billing tasks so they can spend more time focused on patient care. We are building our capabilities in medical transcription, clinical documentation, medical coding, medical billing, and revenue cycle management.

Our founder, Zeeshan Zahid Gill, brought experience in clinical laboratory work, medical transcription, quality assurance, and CPC and CPB training to the company. That understanding of both healthcare and documentation continues to shape how we work: We listen to each client's requirements, build processes around their systems, and give every report and claim the attention it deserves.

Zeeshan Zahid Gill, Founder & CEO

Clinical Documentation Built Around Your Workflow

Capital Links has supported healthcare clients in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, and Saudi Arabia. Our transcriptionists, editors and quality assurance professionals work across specialties including Family Medicine, Cardiology, Psychiatry, Orthopedics, Pathology, and Radiology. They are experienced with different accents, dictation styles, templates, and turnaround time (TAT) requirements.

We can work directly within a client's EMR or EHR system or provide access to a subscription-based dictation and documentation platform. As clinical documentation technology evolves, our team also reviews and edits voice recognition and AI-generated drafts, combining the speed of new tools with professional oversight.

Medical Coding, Billing, and Revenue Cycle Management

Capital Links's coding and billing services help healthcare organizations manage the financial journey from patient registration through final payment. Our team of AAPC certified coders and billers supports insurance verification and eligibility, prior authorizations, provider credentialing, medical coding, charge entry, claims preparation and submission, denial management, payment posting, accounts receivable follow-up, and billing audits and reporting.

Accurate documentation and coding are essential to a well-run revenue cycle. We work to identify issues early, follow up on outstanding claims, and give clients a clearer view of their billing operations. Our goal is to help practices reduce avoidable delays and protect the revenue they have earned while their teams remain focused on patient care.

Investing in Pakistan's Healthcare Workforce

Our commitment extends beyond client services. Through a strategic partnership with the American Academy of Professional Coders (AAPC), Capital Links is supporting professional training opportunities in Pakistan for the Certified Professional Coder (CPC®) and Certified Professional Biller (CPB®) credentials.

Capital Links is helping students access these programs by coordinating enrollment, training schedules, payments, and required learning resources with AAPC. This initiative reflects our long-term aim to develop a skilled, qualified workforce in Pakistan for the global business of healthcare.
