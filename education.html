Questions about AAPC certification

Who teaches the courses? ›

Courses are taught live online by AAPC-certified instructors and faculty.

Is the training online or in person? ›

The training is 100% live online, allowing you to learn from anywhere with AAPC faculty.

Who awards the certification? ›

The certification is awarded directly by AAPC upon passing the official exam.

What does Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription do? ›

As AAPC's Strategic Partner in Pakistan, Capital Links supports students throughout enrollment, batch coordination, payment processing, and access to required books and learning materials.

Which course should I choose? ›

If you want to focus on medical coding, choose CPC®. For medical billing, choose CPB®. If you want comprehensive knowledge across both, choose the CPC® + CPB® dual certification package.

How much does it cost? ›

CPC® is USD 1,050, CPB® is USD 1,050, and the dual CPC® + CPB® package is USD 1,800 (saving USD 300).

Do I need a medical background? ›

No prior medical background is required. The courses cover fundamental medical terminology and anatomy as part of the curriculum.

How do I register for the CPC exam? ›

Capital Links coordinates your exam registration directly with AAPC as part of your course package.

How do I maintain my certification? ›

Certifications are maintained by earning Continuing Education Units (CEUs) through AAPC membership.
