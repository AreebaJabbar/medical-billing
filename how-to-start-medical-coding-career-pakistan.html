How to start a medical coding career from Pakistan

By AAPC Pakistan Strategic Team • August 27, 2026 • 7 min read

What medical coders do, the skills US employers look for, how long it takes to become job-ready, and a realistic path from beginner to certified.

US healthcare providers heavily outsource medical billing and coding operations to certified professionals in Pakistan. As the official AAPC Strategic Partner in Pakistan, Capital Links provides a direct roadmap for students and professionals to gain internationally recognized credentials.

Why Choose US Medical Coding in Pakistan?

Medical coding offers remote flexibility, lucrative compensation paid in foreign exchange equivalencies, and rapid career advancement. Demand for AAPC Certified Professional Coders (CPC®) in Pakistan has grown exponentially over the past three years.

Official Strategic Partner

Capital Links offers AAPC-approved online instructor-led courses with official AAPC textbooks, practice exams, and local Pakistan exam voucher access.

4-Step Pathway to Becoming Certified

Step 1: Master Medical Terminology & Anatomy

Understand medical prefixes, root words, suffixes, human anatomy, and physiological systems necessary to interpret physician chart notes.

Step 2: Learn ICD-10-CM, CPT & HCPCS Level II

Master code lookup rules, guidelines, modifier applications, and Medicare coverage determinations across inpatient and outpatient specialties.

Step 3: Complete AAPC CPC® Instructor-Led Training

Participate in live interactive online lectures with AAPC-licensed instructors, complete weekly coding drills, and solve real clinical scenarios.

Step 4: Pass the AAPC CPC® Exam

Take the 4-hour, 100-question AAPC examination online or at designated testing centers in Pakistan with full support from Capital Links.
