Certified Professional Coder (CPC)®

Prepare for the CPC® exam, AAPC's credential for physician and outpatient medical coding, through AAPC's live, instructor-led online course.

Format: Online sessions conducted by AAPC certified trainers
Duration: 16 weeks, live online sessions of 1.5 hours per week, plus optional one-on-one virtual time with your instructor

What is a Certified Professional Coder?

The CPC credential validates a professional's comprehensive knowledge of medical coding across outpatient and physician services. Earning the CPC demonstrates proficiency in applying ICD-10-CM, CPT®, and HCPCS Level II codes, as well as expertise in compliance, payer rules, and reimbursement processes.

As the most respected medical coding certification in the healthcare industry, the CPC sets the standard for coding excellence. It is the best place to start your coding career because it builds the foundation you'll need for any coding path you choose.

Why earn the CPC certification?

  • Gain the most recognized medical coding credential in global healthcare.
  • Validate your ability to code outpatient services, procedures, and diagnoses with accuracy and compliance.
  • Build a solid foundation in medical terminology, anatomy, and coding guidelines.
  • Increase your earning potential and broaden your career opportunities in coding and auditing.
  • Stepping stone to advanced certifications such as specialty coding or compliance.

What does the CPC cover?

Core Coding Knowledge

Applying ICD-10-CM, CPT®, and HCPCS Level II codes across body systems and procedure types.

Outpatient Services

Evaluation and management (E/M) coding, physician practices, clinics, and surgical centers.

Compliance & Payers

Medicare, Medicaid, commercial rules, modifiers, fraud prevention, and HIPAA compliance.

Who should earn the CPC?

  • Healthcare professionals and billers
  • Those looking to launch their career in medical coding with the industry's leading credential
  • Experienced but uncertified professionals seeking to validate their skills and boost earning potential
  • Healthcare staff transitioning into medical coding roles from administrative or clinical support positions
  • Professionals planning long-term growth who want a foundation that leads to advanced or specialty certifications

Experience requirements

Training requires a high-level knowledge of medical terminology, anatomy, and pathophysiology. If you don't have medical experience or working knowledge of these subjects, AAPC offers prerequisite courses to prepare you for certification training.

The certification exam will test your understanding of the proper application of CPT® procedure codes, HCPCS Level II procedure and supply codes, and ICD-10-CM diagnosis codes.

Maintaining your certification

To maintain your credential, you must maintain your AAPC annual membership, and earn 36 continuing education units (CEUs) every two years.

Expand your opportunities with the CPC certification.

Becoming a CPC-certified professional demonstrates your readiness to succeed in the most in-demand area of healthcare administration. This credential proves your ability to apply coding guidelines across a wide range of services and specialties, ensuring accurate reimbursement and compliance.

The CPC also serves as the launching point for a long-term career path — providing the base knowledge you'll use to pursue advanced coding certifications, auditing, compliance, or specialty expertise. Whether you're entering the field, validating your experience, or preparing to expand your career options, the CPC is your pathway to recognition, professional growth, and higher earning potential.

Questions about CPC®

Who teaches the courses? ›

Training is delivered online by AAPC. Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription is AAPC's Strategic Partner in Pakistan.

Is the training online or in person? ›

The training is conducted 100% online through live interactive sessions, allowing students across Pakistan to attend conveniently.

Who awards the certification? ›

Certification is awarded directly by AAPC upon successfully passing the official CPC examination.

What does Capital Links Medical Billing & Transcription do? ›

As AAPC's Strategic Partner in Pakistan, Capital Links supports students throughout enrollment, fee payment processing, course book delivery, and batch scheduling.

Which course should I choose? ›

Choose CPC for outpatient medical coding (ICD-10-CM, CPT®, HCPCS Level II). Choose CPB for medical billing and reimbursement, or opt for the CPC + CPB dual certification for complete revenue cycle expertise.

How much does it cost? ›

The CPC course package is USD 1,050. This covers official AAPC instructor-led training, 6 months Blackboard access, 1-year AAPC membership, 2 exam attempts, and official required course books.

Do I need a medical background? ›

While a healthcare background is beneficial, it is not required. AAPC offers prerequisite courses in medical terminology and anatomy to prepare students for the CPC training course.

How do I register for the CPC exam? ›

Once you complete your training, you can schedule your online CPC examination directly through the AAPC student portal at a time convenient for you.

How do I maintain my certification? ›

To maintain your CPC credential, you must maintain an active AAPC annual membership and earn 36 continuing education units (CEUs) every two years.
